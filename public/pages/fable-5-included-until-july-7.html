What "Included until July 7" means for Fable 5

Decoding the badge and the black tooltip in the Claude model picker: a promotional access window, a 50% weekly-limit cap, and a faster usage burn rate.

TL;DR

  • Fable 5 is bundled with paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, select Enterprise) at no extra cost — but only through July 7, 2026.
  • During this window it can consume at most 50% of your weekly usage limit; the other half stays reserved for other models.
  • Fable 5 draws down usage roughly 2× faster than Opus 4.8, in line with its API pricing.
  • After July 7 (or after hitting the cap), Fable 5 runs on prepaid usage credits at metered rates instead of plan usage.

Overview

Both UI elements in the screenshot — the grey "Included until July 7" badge next to the model name, and the black tooltip about weekly limits — describe the same thing: a temporary promotional access window for Fable 5 inside paid Claude subscriptions.1

Fable 5 became available to users globally on July 1, 2026, on the Claude Platform, Claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork.1 The badge tells you how long the bundled access lasts; the tooltip tells you the two conditions attached to it while it lasts.

"Included until July 7"

The badge means Fable 5 currently comes bundled with your subscription at no extra cost, but only through July 7, 2026. For Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise plans, Fable 5 is included for up to 50% of weekly usage limits through July 7, after which it becomes available via usage credits.1

In other words: right now, using Fable 5 draws from the same weekly allowance your plan already gives you. After July 7, selecting Fable 5 no longer touches that allowance at all — it consumes a separate, prepaid credit balance instead (see After July 7).

The black tooltip, decoded

The tooltip — "You can use up to 50% of your weekly limits on Fable 5, then it runs on usage credits. Fable 5 draws down usage much faster than Opus 4.8." — packs in the two caveats that apply even during the included window.

1 · The 50% cap

You can use Fable 5 for up to 50% of your weekly usage limit; after that you can switch to another model for the remainder of your usage, or continue on Fable 5 via usage credits.2 Only half of the weekly allowance can go to Fable 5 — the rest is reserved for other models unless you opt into paying with credits.

Weekly usage limit · how it can be allocated (through July 7)

2 · Faster drawdown

Fable 5 consumes your usage allowance at a much higher rate than Opus 4.8.5 That tracks its API pricing, which sits at roughly double Opus 4.8's on both input and output ($10 / $50 per million tokens for Fable 5).4 Inside subscription plans, Fable 5 has been reported to count roughly twice Opus's weight toward limits — so the same session burns through your weekly allowance about 2× as fast.5

Combined effect: the 50% cap and the ~2× burn rate stack. Heavy users can exhaust the Fable 5 share of a weekly limit well before July 7 arrives, making the effective free window shorter than six days.4

After July 7: usage credits

Once the window closes (or once you hit the 50% cap early), Fable 5 switches to usage credits — Anthropic's metered, prepaid billing mechanism. Credits draw down at per-token rates rather than counting against plan limits.24

The confirmed API rate for Fable 5 is $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens — about twice Opus 4.8.4 What "50% of weekly usage limits" converts to in concrete messages, and how far a given credit balance stretches, varies by plan and workload; your own usage dashboard is the reliable place to check.4

Mobile-store note: if the subscription was purchased through a mobile app store, usage credits can only be enabled and purchased on the web version of Claude, not in the mobile app.5

How we got here

The short window is a product of Fable 5's unusual launch history: released in June, suspended under a US export-control directive on June 12 following a reported safeguard bypass, then redeployed globally on July 1 with new classifiers — and a fresh, six-day included window.13

Practical upshot

Through July 7, Fable 5 can be used on a paid plan without extra cost — but budget for it burning the weekly limit roughly twice as fast as Opus 4.8, and capping out at half of it.25 After July 7, either switch back to Opus 4.8 (or Sonnet) for included usage, or enable usage credits to keep using Fable 5 at metered rates.14
